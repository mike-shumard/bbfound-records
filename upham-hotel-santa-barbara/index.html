Facts from public sources

Property typeHotel established in 1871 with 50 rooms, including garden cottages and suites, in 19th-century redwood buildings. [1][2]
Address1404 De La Vina Street, Santa Barbara, CA 93101 (downtown Santa Barbara). [1]
RoomsThree categories: cottages, suites and rooms. Many garden cottages and select suites have fireplaces; cottage room types list a private patio or garden porch. Listed room amenities include air-conditioning, coffee maker, refrigerator and Wi-Fi. [2][3]
AmenitiesComplimentary high-speed Wi-Fi, 24-hour front desk, on-site restaurant (Bistro Amasa, adjacent to the lobby), meeting rooms and gardens. [1][3]
Breakfast / diningThe official site does not state that breakfast is included. The restaurant page for Bistro Amasa mentions lunch and dinner; hours vary seasonally. [3][4]
ParkingLimited complimentary on-site parking, plus street parking. Accessible parking is listed among hotel facilities. [1][3]
AccessibilityADA-compliant guest rooms, including an ADA Garden Cottage Fireplace King with accessible paths and showers. [3]
Location factsPer the official FAQ: about 1.5 miles from the coastline; about 1.2 miles from the Santa Barbara Amtrak station; 12 miles from Santa Barbara Airport; near MTD bus lines. [3]
Check-in / check-outCheck-in 4:00 pm; check-out 11:00 am. [1][3]
Contact(805) 962-0058; innkeeper@uphamhotel.com [1]
