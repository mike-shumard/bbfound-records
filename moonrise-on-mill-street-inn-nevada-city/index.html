Facts from public sources

Property typeTwo rental units on one property sharing a courtyard: The Residence (an 1890 Folk Victorian house) and The Carriage Haus. Opened as an inn in 2023. [1][4]
Address107 Mill St, Nevada City, CA 95959, half a block off Broad Street in downtown Nevada City. [2][5]
UnitsThe Residence: 3 bedrooms, 2 baths, sleeps 7. The Residence one-bedroom (ground floor only): sleeps 2; typically offered weekdays year-round and off-season weekends. The Carriage Haus: 1 bedroom, 1 bath, sleeps 3 to 4, three stories. Entire property: 4 bedrooms, 3 baths, sleeps 11. [1][2][3]
AmenitiesFull kitchen, natural gas fireplace, air conditioning, washer and dryer, Wi-Fi (700+ Mbps), TV with Roku, and a shared courtyard. [1][2][3]
Breakfast / diningBreakfast is not stated on the official site. Units are self-catering with full kitchens. [2][3]
ParkingThe Residence one-bedroom listing states parking is included. The Carriage Haus has no parking (street parking only; the official site notes free parking on Spring St. at Mill St.). [2][3]
Pet policyDogs allowed with prior agreement to dog rules and a non-refundable fee; no cats. [2][3]
AccessibilityThe Residence one-bedroom is single level with two small porch steps and is not ADA compliant. The Carriage Haus has two flights of steep stairs with the bedroom on the third floor; the official site states it is not suitable for guests with mobility issues. [2][3]
Check-in / check-outCheck-in 4:00 pm; check-out 11:00 am (per the one-bedroom listing). [2]
PoliciesNo smoking on the property. The Carriage Haus charges $25 per night for each guest over 4. Wildfire policy: no refunds or rescheduling for fire-related issues unless a mandatory government evacuation order prevents access. [2][3]
Contact(530) 264-8810; moonriseonmillst@gmail.com [5]
