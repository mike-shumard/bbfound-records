Facts from public sources

Property typeBed and breakfast on 3.5 acres. The current house was built in 1932 on the site of the John Cyrus Ranch (established in the 1860s) and became a bed and breakfast after a 1985 sale. [1][3]
Address2653 Foothill Blvd., Calistoga, CA 94515. Foothill Blvd. is the continuation of Highway 128; the inn is north of Petrified Forest Road. [1][6]
Rooms4 guest rooms and 1 guest cottage (5 units). Queen beds, private baths with claw-foot tubs (one room's private bathroom is detached, across the hall), air conditioning, TV and bathrobes. One first-floor room has a private exterior entrance. The cottage has a living room with a queen-size sofa bed and a freestanding Buck stove. [2]
AmenitiesHeated swimming pool, spa/hot tub, pool table, parlor with hearth, gardens and a small vineyard. [1][5]
Breakfast / diningFull breakfast with a homemade hot entree and eggs produced on the property. Complimentary afternoon hors d'oeuvres and beverages from local wineries. [1][4]
Location factsThe official site describes the inn as a few minutes from downtown Calistoga. [5]
Cancellation policyCancellation more than 5 days before the reservation date: $50 fee. Cancellation 5 days or less before: 50% of the total reservation. [2]
Pet policyNot stated on the official site. A third-party pet directory (BringFido) states that dogs are allowed in the 1-bedroom cottage only; this has not been confirmed by the property. [7]
Contact707-942-5534; bearflag@pobox.com [1]
