Facts from public sources

Property typeHotel with 12 rooms; a member of the Kirkwood Collection. [1]
Address421 S Milpas St, Santa Barbara, CA 93103. The official site states the hotel is steps from East Beach. [1]
RoomsRooms have either a king bed or two queen beds. Room types listed include one accessible king room (Cruiser King, Accessible). [1][3]
AmenitiesOutdoor pool with loungers, complimentary glass of champagne on arrival, freshly baked cookies, complimentary filtered water, tea kettle, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi and hair dryer. [1][3]
ParkingThe accessibility disclosures list on-site designated accessible parking, including van-accessible spaces. General guest parking is not described. [4]
Pet policyPet friendly: $40 per night per dog; an additional cleaning charge may apply. Pets may not be left alone in the room ($250 fine). [2]
AccessibilityGround-floor hotel entrance and an ADA accessible guest room with roll-in shower. Rooms above the ground floor are reached by stairs only. [3][4]
Location factsThe official site places the hotel near the Santa Barbara Zoo, the Wharf and the Funk Zone. [1]
Check-in / check-outCheck-in after 4:00 pm (not guaranteed); check-out 11:00 am. [2]
PoliciesRates are for double occupancy; $25 per extra adult. Full payment is taken 7 days before arrival. 7-day cancellation policy (one-night charge for direct bookings cancelled within 7 days). Cash is not accepted. Smoking is prohibited in all guest rooms. Quiet time 10:00 pm to 7:00 am. [2]
