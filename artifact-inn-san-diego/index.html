Facts from public sources

Property typeInn with 12 guest rooms in a remodeled 19th-century lodging building, spread across two buildings (7 rooms in Building A, 5 in Building B). [1][3]
Address828 G Street, San Diego, CA 92101 (northeast corner of 8th Avenue and G Street, downtown San Diego). [1][3]
Rooms12 rooms: 1 Deluxe 2-Bedroom King Suite, 2 Queen Suites, 4 Loft Guest Rooms and 5 Residences (Residences have full kitchens and laundry). All rooms have kitchen and cooking essentials. [1]
Check-in / check-outDigital self check-in; no traditional front desk and no on-site reception staff. Check-in 4:00 pm, check-out 11:00 am. [2][3]
Breakfast / diningThe dining page describes an on-site cafe, The Farmacy Cafe (721 8th Avenue), serving breakfast, lunch and brunch. Breakfast inclusion: see Conflicting information below. [5]
ParkingNo off-street or dedicated parking. Metered street parking (meters enforced 8:00 am to 6:00 pm, Monday to Saturday; free on Sundays and holidays). Self-serve lots on the south side of G Street and at 8th Avenue and F Street. A loading zone in front of the inn may be used at check-in and check-out. [2][3][4]
Pet policyPets are not allowed. Trained service dogs are allowed with advance notice. [2][4]
AccessibilityAll guest rooms are on upper floors; there are no elevators or lifts. The Farmacy Cafe, the courtyard and a ground-floor public restroom are described as ADA-compliant. [2][3]
Location factsPer the official FAQ: Petco Park and the Gaslamp Quarter are several blocks away; the Convention Center and harbor waterfront are less than one mile away. [4]
PoliciesSmoke-free property. Quiet hours 9:00 pm to 8:00 am. Base rate covers 2 guests; additional guests $35 per night. No guests under 18 without parents or prior management approval. Deposit of one night plus 13.5% tax at booking. Cancellation as listed on the policies page (policies vary by booking channel): full refund more than 2 days before arrival; 50% refund within 48 hours of arrival. [2]
Contact(619) 500-1980; hello@theartifactinn.com [1]

Conflicting information on official sources

  • The official FAQ page contains a second, differently formatted answer set stating that a complimentary breakfast buffet is included, that complimentary on-site parking is available and that check-in is at 2:00 pm. These statements conflict with the policies, accessibility and dining pages (no dedicated parking; 4:00 pm check-in; breakfast at the on-site cafe). This record uses the policies and accessibility pages. Breakfast inclusion is not confirmed.
